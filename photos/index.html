Ordering follows what the bench is actually for: the FPGA first, then the things that move and the electronics that drive them, then the supporting parts.

One thing worth flagging. The Verilog in motor/src/motor.v is written against a DRV8833 H-bridge (IN1/IN2 on pins G5/F5), but there is no DRV8833 in these photos. What is here is a DRV8301 three-phase BLDC stage, an L293D DIP H-bridge and a ULN2003 unipolar stepper board. The L293D is the closest drop-in for that code — its two enable pins take the PWM and its direction pins replace IN1/IN2 — while the DRV8301 is a different job entirely and needs six PWM inputs, not two.

Built from 84 phone originals, which are kept outside this repo because they carry GPS EXIF. The published copies in photos/images/ are cropped, stripped of all metadata, and redacted where a delivery slip was in frame. Regenerate with PHOTO_SRC=<dir> python3 photos/build_gallery.py.

Part identifications are read off package markings and board silkscreens in the photos. Resistor values are only stated where the tape is hand-labelled or the bands are unambiguous — the rest are flagged as needing a meter.
